# Career KB — Brihat Jataka, Chapter X: Karmajeeva (Profession)

**Status: source extract, single translation.** No second machine-readable
copy of Brihat Jataka exists — `scripts/audit_kb_sources.py` (re-run for this
extraction, per CLAUDE.md) shows exactly one usable copy: the OCR export of
`PDFs/2015.406251.Brihat-Jataka_text.pdf` (the PDF itself has no text layer —
vision-only). The only other Brihat Jataka-adjacent file in the corpus,
`EPUBS/2015.312156.Jataka-Parijata.epub`, is a *different* text (Vaidyanatha
Dikshita's Jataka Parijata) and is itself near-empty of text (90% images) —
not usable as a second Brihat Jataka witness. This is a single-source
extraction, not a two-agreeing-sources cross-check like
[health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md](health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md) or
[career_kb_bphs_10th_house.md](career_kb_bphs_10th_house.md) — flagged
plainly rather than implied.

**Source**: Varahamihira, *Brihat Jataka*, trans. B. Suryanarain Rao
(Bangalore, 1919). Citations are `BJ <stanza>, p.<printed page>`. The chapter
occupies printed pages 142–145 (PDF pages 143–146 of the OCR'd scan — a
consistent +1 offset confirmed against the book's own table of contents,
which lists "Karma Jeeva or means of livelihood ... 142"). Four stanzas, each
with the translator's own explanatory notes; this is a short, self-contained
chapter, not a fragment of a longer one.

This does not supersede `career_kb_bphs_10th_house.md` — it is additive. Ch.X
uses a **different chart layer** than either of BPHS's two career techniques.

## The finding that should shape the agent

This chapter contains **three distinct techniques**, not one, and only one of
them overlaps with material already in the KB:

1. **10th-house occupant** (Rashi, direct) — new to the KB. Which planet
   physically sits in the 10th from Lagna or Moon.
2. **Navamsa of the 10th lord** — already in the KB via
   [docs/phaladeepika_crosscheck.md](phaladeepika_crosscheck.md)'s Adhyaya V
   extraction (`phal5_navamsa_tenth_lord_livelihood`), summarized there with
   three illustrative planets (Sun, Mercury, Mars). **Brihat Jataka is the
   older, more authoritative source for this exact same technique** —
   Varahamihira (6th century) predates Phaladeepika by roughly a millennium —
   and gives the full seven-graha table where Phaladeepika's extraction only
   illustrated three. This is a genuine convergence between two independent
   classical texts on the same varga-based method, now recorded with full
   detail rather than a sample.
3. **10th lord's house-type (own/friendly/enemy) plus a Sun-exaltation
   special case** — a different, general-purpose rule not previously in the
   KB.

None of the three techniques mention doshas, remedies, or anything requiring
a safety gate — this chapter is entirely descriptive profession-typing, the
lowest-risk kind of career content.

**One methodological note worth preserving on its own** (from the Stanza 1
commentary): the translator explicitly warns against collapsing a person's
livelihood to one planet or one technique. He lists a single person
plausibly being "a lawyer, may have lands yielding produce, may carry on
ready money transactions, may be a paid trustee... and may hold shares in
banks... and be at the same time a Government Pleader" — income from father,
mother, spouse's dowry, and siblings simultaneously — and states that
picking "the stronger" significator and discarding the rest "does not
receive support from eminent writers like Gargi." This is a direct textual
warning against exactly the failure mode a career agent is most likely to
fall into: forcing one deterministic "your profession is X" verdict out of a
chart that legitimately supports several.

## What was handled carefully rather than extracted verbatim

**Saturn's navamsa-lord profession (Stanza 3)** reads in the source: *"he
gets money from labour, such as travelling and carrying, by being an
executioner or hangman, & such mean trades which are against the traditions
of his family."* The astrological content — labor, transport/carrying work,
and enforcement-adjacent occupations — is genuine and extracted below. The
surrounding framing ("mean trades," judged against "the traditions of his
family") is 1919 class-prejudiced editorializing, not an astrological claim,
and is not carried into the reference. This is the same discipline as the
BPHS trade-conduct divergence: extract what the source actually claims about
planetary significance, not the translator's social judgment riding along
with it.

**Mercury's navamsa-lord profession list (Stanza 2)** ends with an
OCR-uncertain word: "...poet, mathematician, architect or scant maker." Given
the Sun's entry two lines earlier reads "scented articles," this is plausibly
a misscan of "scent-maker" — but it is a guess, not a measurement. It is
**omitted** from the extracted list rather than silently corrected, per
CLAUDE.md's instruction to measure rather than remember when a KB source is
image-only or OCR'd. The other seven items in that sentence are unambiguous
and are kept.

## 1. Tenth-house occupant (Stanza 1) — new technique

Method: find the planet occupying the 10th house from Lagna or from the
Moon.

| Occupant of 10th | Source of wealth |
| --- | --- |
| Sun | Father — paternal property, inheritance |
| Moon | Mother |
| Mars | "Through his enemy" |
| Mercury | Friends |
| Jupiter | Brothers |
| Venus | Women |
| Saturn | Servants |

- **BJ St.1, p.142** — if more than one planet occupies the 10th, wealth
  flows through the channels of whichever planet's dasha/antardasha is
  active at the time, not a single fixed source.
- **BJ St.1, p.142** — if no planet occupies the 10th from Lagna or Moon,
  fall through to the navamsa-of-the-10th-lord technique (§2 below) instead.

## 2. Navamsa of the 10th lord (Stanzas 2–3) — full table, converges with Phaladeepika Adhyaya V

Method: locate the 10th lord (from Lagna, Moon, *and* Sun — Stanza 1 names
all three reference points), find which navamsa it occupies, and read the
lord of that navamsa.

| Navamsa lord of the 10th lord | Profession / livelihood type |
| --- | --- |
| Sun | Perfumery/scented articles, gold, wool, medicines — a medical professional or an attendant/nurse |
| Moon | Corals, pearls, shells, agricultural or watery produce; livelihood bound up with women |
| Mars | Minerals/ores, weapons, fire-adjacent work (kitchens, engine-driving, fireworks), adventurous or risk-taking work, occupations using physical strength |
| Mercury | Writer, mechanic, painter, sculptor, engraver, poet, mathematician, architect |
| Jupiter | Brahmins/priests, temples, mining or manufacturing, charitable work, discipleship, pilgrimage-related livelihood |
| Venus | Gems, silver/metals, cattle (cows, buffaloes) |
| Saturn | Labor, transport/carrying work, enforcement-adjacent occupations *(see "handled carefully" above — the source's class-prejudiced framing of this entry is not carried forward)* |

- **BJ St.3, p.142–143** — a Gochara (transit) variant of the same
  technique is also given: track the 10th lord's *current* transiting
  navamsa and read the same table for the presently active income method.
  The translator notes this transit variant applies only to charts with
  known birth data, not to reconstructed/lost-birth-detail charts (Nasta
  Jataka) — not a concern for this app, which always has birth data, but
  recorded for completeness. **Not extracted as a reference** — no dasha/
  gochara-of-navamsa timing feature exists in the engine to consume it; a
  named gap, not a silent one.

## 3. Tenth lord's house-type, and the Sun-exaltation special case (Stanza 4)

- **BJ St.4, p.145** — general rule: the 10th lord's placement determines
  wealth by house-relationship — own house → wealth through that house's own
  domain; friendly house → wealth through friends; unfriendly/enemy house →
  wealth through enemies or opposition.
- **BJ St.4, p.145** — if the Sun specifically is the 10th lord, exalted and
  otherwise strong, the person acquires wealth by **self-exertion /
  self-acquisition** rather than through any of the relationship channels
  above. The translator notes this self-made distinction is called out for
  the Sun specifically, not generalized to other exalted planets.
- **BJ St.4, p.145** — powerful benefics placed in the Lagna, 2nd, or 11th
  house (independent of the 10th lord) → wealth through many different
  channels; the commentator adds that such a person "will succeed in any
  business he undertakes, however divergent it may be from his original
  work" — i.e., versatility/adaptability across income sources, not a single
  fixed-profession reading.

# Health KB — BPHS Chapter 19, read from the printed page

**Status:** source extract, verified. Every rule below was read directly from
rendered page images of the scanned book — not from OCR, not from an LLM
summary. Coverage is stated exactly and is partial; see "What is NOT here".

**Source:** *Brihat Parasara Hora Sastra*, English translation by Girish Chand
Sharma, Volume 1. Chapter 19, अथ षष्ठभावफलाध्यायः ॥१९॥, "Effects of the Sixth
House", book pages 233–236 (PDF pages 241–244).

## Why this document exists, and why it reads paranoid

Three earlier KB/spec documents for this project were generated by an LLM over
this same corpus, and all three contained fabricated specifics. The reason is
now measured rather than suspected: **the corpus is a failed OCR.** The EPUBs
carry a per-page accuracy note, and across 658 pages of this volume the median
is **46%**, with not one flagged page above 60%. The extracted English is
character soup — the OCR for the page facing this chapter reads
`111६ 51111 8॥16 {116 001 21110`. A model handed that will pattern-complete
into plausible astrology, which is exactly what happened.

The scans themselves are excellent: 300 DPI, high contrast, both scripts
legible. So the fix is to *read the page image* rather than its text layer.
That is what this document is.

### Concretely, what reading the page corrected

An LLM summary of this material, presented under the heading "Strictly Grounded
in Your Excerpts (Verified with Verses)", got the following wrong:

| Claim made | What the page says |
| --- | --- |
| Chapter **49** | Chapter **19** — the volume only has 47 chapters |
| Moon → face or throat | Moon → **mouth** |
| Mars → navel (merged with Mercury) | Mars → **neck**; Mercury → navel |
| Venus → eyes or feet | Venus → **eyes** |
| Saturn → stomach (merged with Rahu) | Saturn → **foot**; Rahu/Ketu → stomach |
| A body-map for the native | A rule about **relatives** — see below |
| Leprosy: "Ascendant Lord, Mars, and Mercury" (three planets) | "being Ascendant Lord**s** Mars **or** Mercury" — Mars or Mercury *as* the ascendant lord |
| Fevers: "the 6th **or** 8th Lord" | "the 6th **and** the 8th Lords" |

Four of nine planets wrong, the governing frame wrong, and two conjunction
rules materially altered — all under a heading asserting verse-level grounding.
The dhatu table from the same summary was, separately, exactly right. Nothing
in the response distinguished the two, which is the whole problem.

## 1. Scope of the 6th house (v.1 + Notes, p.233)

> "the effects produced by the 6th House which are diseases ulcers in the body"

Notes enumerate: diseases, obstacles, fighting, swelling, abscesses, ulcers,
wounds, enemies, enmity, debt, loss, consumption, mental tension, chronic eye
disease, poisoning, colicky stomach pain, urinary-tract disease, diarrhoea.

Governing principle, stated in the Notes:

> "Every 6th Lord is a representative of injury or disease. The 6th Lord gives
> disease or injury to the organ related to that house or to the organ related
> to the Lord of that house with the Lord of which he is in conjunction and on
> which house he or on the Lord of which house he has his aspect."

## 2. 6th lord in 6th, 1st or 8th (v.2, p.233)

Ulcer in the limb corresponding to **the sign placed in the 6th house**.

The Notes record an edition variance worth carrying: the **Chaukhamba** edition
begins this sloka `स्वभेषष्ठाधिपः पापो`, i.e. "the *malefic* Sixth Lord" — on
that reading the rule applies when the 6th lord is a malefic, and a malefic 6th
lord intensifies the result. Two defensible readings; flag as
`convention_dependent`.

## 3. Affliction by house-lord — v.3–5 (p.234)

**This is a rule about relatives, not about the native's own body.** The
translation is explicit:

> "the Lords of the Houses of father etc and the significators of the these
> houses being together with the 6th Lord and being placed in the 6th or the
> 8th House, **ulcer is to be predicted to those relatives (father etc.)**"

The planet named selects the **limb**; the house selects **whose**. The Notes
give a worked example: Aries ascendant, 4th lord Moon — if the 6th lord is with
Mercury, or the Moon is in the 6th, or 6th lord and Moon are together in the
8th, then *the mother* is indicated, because the 4th house is the mother's.

| Planet (as such a house lord, in 6th or 8th) | Limb |
| --- | --- |
| Sun (आदित्येन) | head — शिरोव्रणम् |
| Moon (इन्दुना) | mouth — मुखे |
| Mars (भौमेन) | neck — कण्ठे |
| Mercury (ज्ञेन) | navel — नाभिषु |
| Jupiter (गुरुणा) | nose — नासिकायां |
| Venus (भृगुणा) | eyes — नयने |
| Saturn (शनिना) | foot — पदे |
| Rahu (राहुणा) | stomach — कुक्षौ |
| Ketu (केतुना) | stomach — "च तथा भवेत्" (likewise) |

Restated on p.235: "If the Sun is the 6th Lord, or he is together with the 6th
Lord or is placed in the 6th House, or together with the 6th Lord is placed in
the 8th House, then the ulcer will be in the head. If the Moon is there in the
situations of the Sun, then the ulcer will be in the mouth."

## 4. Mars–Mercury as the injury pair (v.6 + Notes, p.235–236)

Verse: ascendant lord in Mars's signs (Aries, Scorpio) **or** Mercury's signs
(Gemini, Virgo), aspected by Mercury → ulcer on the face.

The Notes are the substantive part, and they are the translator's reasoning
rather than the verse:

- Mars signifies **injury**; in the natural zodiac Mercury is the 6th lord and
  so carries the 6th house's quality — hence Mercury signifies **ulcer**.
- "The combination of Mars and Mercury is the greatest factor of giving ulcer".
- For **Gemini** ascendant, Mars rules the 6th and, by *Bhavat Bhavam*, the 6th
  from the 6th — described as making Mars "extremely malefic" for that lagna.
- Mars in Gemini aspecting Mercury → repeated injury to head, hands, shoulders,
  collarbone, neck, nose.
- Mars in the 4th in Virgo → accidents involving conveyances (4th = conveyance,
  6th = injury).

## 5. Kushtha combinations (v.7–8½, p.236)

Recorded for completeness of the source. **See the safety note below — this must
not be surfaced.**

- Mars **or** Mercury *as ascendant lord*, conjunct Moon, Rahu and Saturn → the
  condition is indicated.
- Ascendant lord absent from the ascendant, with Moon and Rahu there → श्वेत
  (white) variety.
- With Saturn → कृष्ण (black); with Mars → रक्त (red).

## 6. Fever, tumour and the absence-of-disease clause (v.9–12½, p.236)

Ascendant occupied by **the 6th and the 8th lords together** —

| …along with | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Sun | ज्वरगण्ड — acute fever |
| Mars | tumour, or ulcer caused by weapon (शस्त्रव्रण) |
| Mercury | bilious (pitta) disorders |
| Jupiter | **रोगाभावं — absence of disease** |

The Jupiter clause matters disproportionately: it is a protective, mitigating
rule stated in the same breath as the afflictive ones, and any summary that
carries the afflictions without it misrepresents the passage.

Verse 11–12½ continues into further combinations (Venus, Moon, Ketu, phlegm)
and extends the same logic to "पित्रादिभावानां" — the houses of father etc.,
i.e. relatives again.

## Engine notes

- **D6 is not a BPHS division.** The contents page for Chapter 7, "The Sixteen
  Divisions of a Sign" (p.97), lists Rasi, Hora, Decanate, Chaturthamsa,
  Sapthamsa, Navamsa, Dasamsa, Dwadasamsa, Shodasamsa, Vimsamsa, Siddhamsa,
  Bhamsa, Trimsamsa, Chatvarimsamsa, Akshavedamsa, Shashtiamsa — it steps
  straight from D4 to D7. `vargas.py`'s `VERIFY` marker on D6 is therefore not
  an open task; there is no verse to find. The health domain currently lists
  **D6 as a primary varga**, which this text does not support. Either demote it
  to supporting behind D30, or mark it `convention_dependent`.
- The rules above are **house-lord and conjunction** rules. They need no new
  engine capability: house lords, occupancy, aspect and conjunction all exist.

## Safety — read before wiring any of this in

This chapter is the sharpest case in the whole corpus for the output net.

1. **It names conditions by archaic diagnostic labels** (kushtha, consumption).
   These must never reach a reader. CLAUDE.md forbids diagnosis outright, and
   `safety.py`'s health patterns already gate the input side.
2. **Most of its rules are about third parties** — father, mother, elder
   brother. That is precisely the gap `_PROHIBITED_OUTPUT` had until PR #21:
   its death and health patterns were anchored to "you". **Do not ingest §3 or
   §6's relative-extension until the third-party output net is merged.** A rule
   whose literal output is "your mother will have an ulcer" is the exact shape
   that guardrail exists to stop.
3. The **Jupiter absence-of-disease clause (§6)** should travel with any
   afflictive rule from that verse. Carrying the affliction alone turns a
   balanced passage into a fear-generating one.

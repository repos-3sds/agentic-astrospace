## The finding that should shape the agent

**The 10th house is not "the career house" in BPHS.** It is the house of
*karma* — acts, deeds — and it is bound up with **the father** at least as much
as with profession. Chapter 21's very first substantive rule is titled
**"PATERNAL HAPPINESS"**:

> **S 21.2, p.137** — a strong 10th lord, exalted or in own rashi/navamsha →
> extreme paternal happiness, fame, and good deeds.

and the same pairing recurs in the lord series:

> **S 24.114** — 10th lord in the 6th → "bereft of paternal bliss."

Santhanam addresses the ambiguity directly in his note on 21.2, observing that
although *karma* means "deed", in this context it carries the professional
sense. Both readings are in the text; neither is a mistake.

Two consequences for how we use this:

1. A career agent that maps "10th house → job" and stops will produce readings
   that are thinner than the source, and will silently discard the father
   material — which our Personality/family domains actually want.
2. Several 10th-lord placements are **not about work at all** (see the table).
   Forcing them into a career frame is the same error as reading every
   6th-lord placement as illness, which is documented in
   [health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md](health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md). The two chapters
   fail the same way for the same reason: a house is a *significator cluster*,
   not a topic label.

## Chapter 21 — Effects of the Tenth House

### Strength of the 10th lord — the spine of the chapter
- **S 21.2, p.137** — 10th lord strong, exalted or in own rashi/navamsha →
  paternal happiness, fame, good deeds.
- **S 21.3, p.137** — 10th lord devoid of strength → **obstructions in one's
  work**. (Separately in the same shloka: Rahu in an angle or trine → performs
  religious sacrifices such as Jyotishtoma.)
- **S 21.4, p.137** — 10th lord with a benefic, or in an auspicious house →
  gain through royal patronage and in business. Contrary placement, contrary
  result.
- **S 21.18, p.140** — 10th lord in fall, with malefics occupying both the 10th
  from the ascendant *and* the 10th from the 10th (i.e. the 7th) →
  obstructions to one's acts.

> The 10th-from-the-10th check in 21.18 is worth noting as technique: the
> chapter reasons about a house *from another house*, not only from the
> ascendant. Our bundle already computes bhava-from-bhava, so this is
> expressible.

### Elevation combinations
- **S 21.8–10, p.138** — 10th lord exalted and with Jupiter, 9th lord in the
  10th → honour, wealth, valour. 11th lord in the 10th with the 10th lord in
  the ascendant, **or** 10th and 11th lords conjunct in an angle → a happy life.
- **S 21.13, p.139** — 10th lord in the 11th, 11th lord in the ascendant, Venus
  in the 10th → endowed with precious stones.
- **S 21.19–21, p.140 — COMBINATIONS FOR FAME**:
  - Moon in the 10th, 10th lord in a trine from the 10th, ascendant lord in an
    angle from the ascendant; **or**
  - 11th lord in the 10th while the 10th lord is strong and aspected by Jupiter.

### Affliction combinations
- **S 21.5, p.138** — malefics occupying both the 10th and the 11th → bad deeds;
  defiles his own people.
- **S 21.6, p.138** — 10th lord in the 8th with Rahu → bad deeds. Santhanam's
  note is the usable part: this **spoils professional happiness**, over and
  above the character claim.
- **S 21.17, p.140** — 10th lord in the 8th while the 8th lord is in the 10th
  with a malefic → indulges in bad acts.

> **S 21.7, p.138** is recorded but not for use: Saturn, Mars and the 10th lord
> in the 7th with an afflicted 7th lord → *Sisnodara Parayana*, given to carnal
> appetite. It is a character verdict with no professional content, and
> Santhanam himself hedges the translation ("as far as I understand"). Not
> servable.

## Chapter 24.109–120 — the 10th lord through the twelve houses

| 10th lord in | Effect (S 24) | Career-bearing? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | scholarly, famous | yes |
| 2nd | wealthy, virtuous, honoured | partly |
| 3rd | happiness from brothers | **no** |
| 4th | happy; always interested (in his pursuits) | partly |
| 5th | endowed with all kinds of (fortune) | partly |
| 6th | **bereft of paternal bliss** | **no — father** |
| 7th | endowed with happiness (marital) | **no** |
| 8th | devoid of good acts; long-lived; blames others | partly |
| 9th | one born of royal scion becomes a king | yes |
| 10th | **skilful in all jobs** | yes |
| 11th | wealth, happiness, sons; virtuous, truthful, delighted | partly |
| 12th | spends through royal abodes | partly |

**Four of twelve are the strongest career signals** — 1st, 9th, 10th, and the
negative reading of the 8th. Three are not about work at all. This is the same
distribution the 6th lord showed for health, and it is the reason both extracts
say the same thing: read the placement, do not assume the topic.

> **Extraction note.** Shloka 119 is absent from a `grep` for "If the 10th lord
> is in the" because the text layer renders it `If-the` with a hyphen. Clean
> text is not the same as uniform text — a single exact-phrase pattern
> under-reports, and the section heading ("up to shloka 120") is what caught it.
> The 6th-lord series had the mirror-image problem: there the heading was wrong
> and the text was right.

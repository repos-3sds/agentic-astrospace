## The finding that should shape the agent

**The 10th house is not "the career house" in BPHS.** It is the house of
*karma* — acts, deeds — and it is bound up with **the father** at least as much
as with profession. Chapter 21's (S) / 23's (G) very first substantive rule is
titled **"PATERNAL HAPPINESS"** in Santhanam and unnamed but identically
themed in Sharma:

> **S 21.2, p.137 / G 23.2** — a strong 10th lord, exalted or in own
> rashi/navamsha → extreme paternal happiness, fame, and good deeds.

and the same pairing recurs in the lord series:

> **S 24.114 / G 26.114** — 10th lord in the 6th → "bereft of paternal
> bliss" (S) / "bereft of paternal bliss, be bereft of wealth and be
> troubled by enemies, inspite of being skilful" (G, fuller).

Both translators agree the 10th House genuinely covers father as much as
livelihood — Sharma's own translator's note on 23.2 is explicit: "The Tenth
House is studied to have the knowledge of the native's good and evil deeds,
his influence, father, fame, his obtaining a governmental office... In the
same way, the means of livelihood... are also studied from the 10th House."

Two consequences for how we use this, unchanged from the single-source
version of this doc:

1. A career agent that maps "10th house → job" and stops will produce readings
   that are thinner than the source, and will silently discard the father
   material — which our Personality/family domains actually want.
2. Several 10th-lord placements are **not about work at all** (see the table).
   Forcing them into a career frame is the same error as reading every
   6th-lord placement as illness, which is documented in
   [health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md](health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md). The two chapters
   fail the same way for the same reason: a house is a *significator cluster*,
   not a topic label.

## What the cross-check actually found

Applying the same two-agreeing-sources bar the health domain's 6th-house
cross-check proved out — read the second translation independently, then
compare — on this chapter produced three kinds of result, not one:

### 1. Full agreement on the rules Santhanam's extraction already had

Every rule previously extracted from Santhanam (S21.2–4, 5, 8–10, 13, 17,
18, 19–21, and the 109–118/120 lord-in-house rows) is confirmed, sense for
sense, in Sharma. No contradictions found on any previously-servable rule.

### 2. New material — rules Santhanam's own extraction had skipped

The existing extraction organised the chapter around three headings ("spine",
"elevation", "affliction") and, in doing so, silently left out several
shlokas that don't fit those buckets cleanly. Reading Sharma's complete
chapter surfaced them:

- **G 23.11** — Sun conjunct Rahu, Mars and Saturn in the 11th House → the
  native fails in the performance of his duties. Sharma's note: the affliction
  lands on the Sun as significator of "mind and soul," costing self-confidence
  and will-power, so "his efforts will generally remain incomplete." A real,
  distinct combination — not in the S-only extraction at all.
- **G 23.16** — Saturn together with a debilitated planet in the 10th House,
  and the 10th house's navamsha also afflicted by a malefic → the native is
  "bereft of actions." Also absent from the S-only extraction.

Two further shlokas (**G 23.14, 15**) are read but deliberately **not**
extracted as career rules — see "Read but not career-bearing" below.

### 3. Sharma's translator's notes add profession-specific detail Santhanam's don't

This is the single most useful find. Santhanam's ch.24 extraction is a bare
one-line-per-house table. Sharma's equivalent series (109–120) carries the
same core rule *plus* a translator's note naming actual **profession types**
for several placements, and three worked historical examples:

| 10th lord in | S (bare rule) | G's added profession-type note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3rd | happiness from brothers | "In the Capricorn Ascendant this combination prompts the native to become an engineer." |
| 4th | happy; interested in pursuits | "the native mostly adopts a profession relating to automobiles, building material, agriculture, dairy etc." |
| 5th | endowed with all fortune | statesmen, scholars, professors, writers, educationists, actors — **worked example: Rabindranath Tagore's horoscope** (10th lord Jupiter exalted in the 5th, Pisces ascendant). |
| 6th | bereft of paternal bliss | "This combination is auspicious only for Doctors and lawyers. In the case of men of other professions, many fluctuations are experienced." |
| 8th | devoid of good acts; long-lived | "If the native adopts the profession of dealing in scraps, mineral substances, L.I.C., leather goods, etc. he will get special success, otherwise he fails badly." |
| 9th | born of royal scion, becomes king | royal/governmental power, professional success — **worked example: John F. Kennedy's horoscope** (10th lord Mercury in the 9th, conjunct 9th lord Venus, Rajayoga). |

A fourth worked example, the actor **Shivaji Ganesan** (10th lord Mars in the
5th house), corroborates the 5th-house profession cluster independently of
the Tagore chart.

**These are genuinely new, citable material** — not a repeat of anything in
the S-only extraction — and they are exactly the kind of concrete,
placement-specific detail a reading needs instead of a bare disposition word.

### 4. One real divergence in emphasis, not a contradiction

**S 21.7 / G 23.7** — Saturn, Mars and the 10th lord in the 7th house, with
an afflicted 7th lord → *Sisnodara Parayana*, given to carnal appetite. The
existing S-only extraction marked this "recorded but not for use... a
character verdict with no professional content," noting Santhanam himself
hedges the translation.

Sharma's note reframes the *same* combination through a professional lens
the S-only extraction never surfaced: **"The Seventh is the House of trade
and business. If this house is occupied by a malefic planet, the native
will adopt illegal means in his trade... The tendency to indulge in
unlawful, immoral deeds will be increased when Saturn and Mars will have
conjunction with the 10th Lord in the 7th House, the house of trade and
business."**

Neither translator is wrong — this is the same pattern the health cross-check
found with मुख (mukha, "face" vs "mouth"): a genuinely ambiguous source term
(here, the character/conduct framing vs. the trade/business framing of the
same affliction) that both translators resolve differently. **Servable now,
reframed**: this combination can be cited for a *conduct-in-business* caution
(illegal/unethical trade practices) — a real professional signal Santhanam's
extraction discarded as pure character content. It must not be cited as a
sexual-character verdict in either translation; that reading stays excluded
exactly as the original extraction decided.

### 5. Read but not career-bearing — excluded on purpose

**G 23.12** (Jupiter and Venus in Pisces, Moon exalted → learned, wealthy,
"enlightened," a philosophical gloss on *samyak jnana*/Buddha-nature) and
**G 23.14–15** (10th lord in angle/trine + exalted + aspected by Jupiter, or
10th lord in the Ascendant with the Ascendant lord and Moon in angle/trine →
"endowed with worthy deeds," a "Karma Yogi") are all real rules in the
chapter, all about *deeds* in the broad karma sense the chapter's own scope
covers — but none of them name profession, livelihood, or any
professionally-actionable outcome. Consistent with this doc's own stated
principle (a house is a significator cluster, not a topic label pressed into
service), these are recorded here so the exclusion is a decision, not a gap,
but they are not added to the servable rule list below.

### 6. One note excluded for a different reason: health content inside a career shloka

**G 23.109**'s translator's note (10th lord in the Ascendant) drifts into
health/longevity claims per-Ascendant — "will have adverse effect on the
native's health... diminution of his longevity" (Aries), "may be troubled by
blood disorders" (Cancer). These clauses are **not extracted here**. They are
health-domain content sitting inside a career chapter's commentary, and
belong — if anywhere — to the health KB's own gated-medical class
([health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md](health_kb_bphs_6th_house.md)), not to a career
reading. The non-health portion of 23.109 (learned, famous, poet, wealth
increasing) is extracted normally below.

## Chapter 21 (S) / 23 (G) — Effects of the Tenth House

Citations: `S 21.<shloka>, p.<page>` / `G 23.<shloka>`. All `[safe]` — none of
this chapter names a disease, a lifespan, or death.

### Strength of the 10th lord — the spine of the chapter
- **S 21.2, p.137 / G 23.2** — 10th lord strong, exalted or in own
  rashi/navamsha → paternal happiness, fame, good deeds.
- **S 21.3, p.137 / G 23.3** — 10th lord devoid of strength → obstructions in
  one's work. (Same shloka: Rahu in an angle or trine → performs religious
  sacrifices such as Jyotishtoma.)
- **S 21.4, p.137 / G 23.4** — 10th lord with a benefic, or in an auspicious
  house → gain through royal patronage and in business. Contrary placement,
  contrary result.
- **S 21.18, p.140 / G 23.18** — 10th lord in fall, with malefics occupying
  both the 10th from the ascendant *and* the 10th from the 10th (i.e. the
  7th) → obstructions to one's acts.

> The 10th-from-the-10th check in 21.18/23.18 is worth noting as technique:
> the chapter reasons about a house *from another house*, not only from the
> ascendant. Our bundle already computes bhava-from-bhava, so this is
> expressible.

### Elevation combinations
- **S 21.8–10, p.138 / G 23.8–10** — 10th lord exalted and with Jupiter, 9th
  lord in the 10th → honour, wealth, valour. 11th lord in the 10th with the
  10th lord in the ascendant, **or** 10th and 11th lords conjunct in an angle
  → a happy life. 10th lord strong, in Pisces, with Jupiter → robes,
  ornaments, happiness (G adds: "there is no doubt in it").
- **S 21.13, p.139 / G 23.13** — 10th lord in the 11th, 11th lord in the
  ascendant, Venus in the 10th → endowed with precious stones.
- **S 21.19–21, p.140 / G 23.19–21 — COMBINATIONS FOR FAME**:
  - Moon in the 10th, 10th lord in a trine from the 10th, ascendant lord in an
    angle from the ascendant; **or**
  - 11th lord in the 10th while the 10th lord is strong and aspected by
    Jupiter; **or**
  - (G, third variant, not previously extracted from S) 10th lord in the 9th
    house, ascendant lord in the 10th, Moon 5th from the ascendant → the
    native becomes "an illustrious person."
- **G 23.11 (new)** — Sun conjunct Rahu, Mars and Saturn in the 11th house →
  native fails in the performance of his duties; loss of self-confidence and
  will-power via affliction to the Sun as significator of mind. A negative
  elevation combination, not previously extracted.

### Affliction combinations
- **S 21.5, p.138 / G 23.5** — malefics occupying both the 10th and the 11th
  → bad deeds; defiles his own people.
- **S 21.6, p.138 / G 23.6** — 10th lord in the 8th with Rahu → bad deeds.
  Santhanam's note: this spoils professional happiness. **G adds (new)**:
  the combination is nonetheless auspicious for specific trades — "the
  leather shoe industry, L.I.C. and Mining industry."
- **S 21.17, p.140 / G 23.17** — 10th lord in the 8th while the 8th lord is
  in the 10th with a malefic → indulges in bad acts.
- **G 23.16 (new)** — Saturn with a debilitated planet in the 10th house, and
  the 10th house's navamsha also afflicted by a malefic → bereft of actions.

> **S 21.7, p.138 / G 23.7** — recorded with a reframed reading; see "One
> real divergence in emphasis" above. Servable only as a trade/business
> conduct caution (Saturn+Mars+10th lord in the 7th with an afflicted 7th
> lord → risk of illegal/unethical trade practice), never as a
> character/sexual-conduct verdict.

## Chapter 24.109–120 (S) / 26.109–120 (G) — the 10th lord through the twelve houses

| 10th lord in | Effect (S, bare) | Career-bearing? | G's added profession-type detail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st | scholarly, famous | yes | learned, famous, poet; wealth increases over time. *(Health/longevity clauses in G's note excluded — see above.)* |
| 2nd | wealthy, virtuous, honoured | partly | "high officials in banks, finance ministers... special governmental authority" (Gemini-lord example) |
| 3rd | happiness from brothers | **no** | Capricorn ascendant → "prompts the native to become an engineer" |
| 4th | happy; always interested | partly | "profession relating to automobiles, building material, agriculture, dairy etc." |
| 5th | endowed with all kinds of (fortune) | partly | statesmen, scholars, professors, writers, educationists, actors — cf. Tagore, Shivaji Ganesan examples |
| 6th | **bereft of paternal bliss** | **no — father** | "auspicious only for Doctors and lawyers"; other professions see fluctuations |
| 7th | endowed with happiness (marital) | **no** | — |
| 8th | devoid of good acts; long-lived; blames others | partly | "dealing in scraps, mineral substances, L.I.C., leather goods" |
| 9th | one born of royal scion becomes a king | yes | royal/governmental power, professional success — cf. Kennedy example |
| 10th | **skilful in all jobs** | yes | valorous, truthful, devoted to teachers/elders (G) |
| 11th | wealth, happiness, sons; virtuous, truthful, delighted | partly | — |
| 12th | spends through royal abodes | partly | spends in royal courts; fear from enemies (G, closely matches S) |

**Four of twelve are the strongest career signals** — 1st, 9th, 10th, and the
negative reading of the 8th. Three are not about work at all. This is the
same distribution the 6th lord showed for health, and it is the reason both
extracts say the same thing: read the placement, do not assume the topic.

Sharma's profession-type notes exist for 2nd, 3rd, 4th, 5th, 6th and 8th —
six of twelve placements now carry a concrete profession or field, not just a
disposition word. This is the servable upgrade this cross-check produces:
where the bundle can compute the 10th lord's house placement, the agent can
now name plausible fields rather than only a temperament.

> **Extraction note (unchanged from the S-only pass).** Shloka 119 is absent
> from a `grep` for "If the 10th lord is in the" against Santhanam's text
> because the layer renders it `If-the` with a hyphen. Clean text is not the
> same as uniform text — a single exact-phrase pattern under-reports, and the
> section heading ("up to shloka 120") is what caught it. Sharma's OCR export
> does not share this artifact; shloka 119 read cleanly from G on the first
> pass.

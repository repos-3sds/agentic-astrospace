Delete your Siddha account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.

In the app

  1. Open Siddha and go to Explore.
  2. Tap the gear to open Settings, then Account & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete account, tick the box, type DELETE and confirm.

Without the app

Email asksiddha@3senses.design from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll reply by email once it's done.

What gets deleted

Your profiles and birth details, charts, readings, Ask questions and answers, notes, saved timings, alerts, preferences, push tokens, email settings and the sign-in account itself. A request log keeps only the fact that a deletion happened, and backups are rotated out on a fixed schedule afterwards.

Deleting only some data

You can keep your account and remove individual profiles, readings, notes and questions inside the app. More detail is in the Privacy Policy.

Questions

Good to know.

Is Siddha a real astrologer?

Siddha is an app. Your chart is calculated exactly from your birth date, time and place, and the readings follow classical texts such as the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra. The words are written by AI and checked against strict rules: plain language, no fear, no verdicts on health, money or legal matters.

How fast do I get an answer in Ask?

Usually within a few minutes. There is no queue and no appointment.

Can Siddha tell me exactly what will happen?

No app or astrologer honestly can. Siddha shows tendencies and good timing, with the reasons, so you can plan calmly. Treat it as guidance, not a guarantee.

Which languages does it support?

English and Telugu today. Mantras can be shown in Telugu, English or Sanskrit script.

Do I need my exact birth time?

An exact time gives the best chart. If you only know it roughly, mark it as approximate. Siddha still works, and your chart shows that the time is approximate.

How do I join the test?

Request an invite with the Gmail address you use on Google Play. We add you to the Android test and send you the link to join.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes, as a test for now. Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the Siddha test link. The app will come to the App Store after the test.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Explore, then Settings, then Account & privacy. Or see how to delete your account without the app. Read our Privacy Policy.
